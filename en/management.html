FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is the management fee?

Essential Operations is 10% of revenue and Full Management is 20% (excluding tax).

What is the difference between the two plans?

In both plans Melglobe handles day-to-day operations such as guest communication and cleaning coordination. The difference is who steers pricing, marketing, listing improvements and revenue decisions: you (Essential Operations) or Melglobe (Full Management).

Can I still control pricing?

Yes. With Essential Operations you keep control of pricing, marketing, listing improvements and revenue decisions.

Do you support owners living abroad?

Yes. We support both owners and guests in Japanese, English and Chinese.

What areas do you cover?

We mainly serve the Kanto (Greater Tokyo) area. We may be able to arrange support elsewhere, so please ask.

How quickly can we start?

Operations can start after a preparation period of as little as one month. We will walk you through the steps once we understand your property.

Do you support licensed properties?

Yes. We work with licensed Airbnb, short-term rental and minpaku properties in Japan, and we can also support you through the licensing application.
