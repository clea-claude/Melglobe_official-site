Main Service

A multilingual web house guide
that keeps guests from getting lost

"What's the Wi-Fi?" "Where does the trash go?" "The AC won't turn on." Are the same questions eating up your time?

Melglobe creates a web house guide made just for your property. Guests scan a QR code in the room and find what they need on the spot. It comes in Japanese, English and Chinese, a chatbot answers questions automatically, and remotes and appliances are explained with labelled photos — so you no longer have to explain the same things again and again.

  • Three languages + chatbot
  • All three languages checked by native speakers
  • Appliance photos with labelled instructions
  • Delivered in two weeks (standard)
